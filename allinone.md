## Summary

### Overview

The search spans 2010–2026 and retained 68 papers across smooth/jerk-bounded trajectory generation, aerial physical interaction, admittance/impedance control, passivity, and terminal attitude control. The corpus is broader than the exact LightBender release problem; the closest directly reusable methods are online jerk-limited target-state generation and predictive/passivity-aware aerial admittance control.

### Trends

Early work centered on physical human–quadrotor admittance and variable impedance (2013–2014). From 2017–2022, the literature added jerk-bounded online trajectory generators and passivity/energy-tank safety. Recent work moves toward predictive admittance and online time-optimal multicopter planning, while many terminal-sliding-mode papers address tracking convergence but not explicitly contact-release overshoot.

### Key themes

1. **Jerk-limited stopping and arbitrary terminal states:** Papers #4, #5, #17, and #51 generate smooth online trajectories under velocity, acceleration, and jerk limits.
2. **Human/aerial physical interaction:** Papers #3, #26, and #49 apply admittance concepts directly to quadrotors or UAVs.
3. **Passivity and energy management:** Papers #18, #19, #37, and #41 constrain interaction energy and stability during uncertain contact.
4. **Variable impedance/admittance:** Papers #1, #39, and #40 adapt compliance or force tracking to changing contact conditions.
5. **Terminal attitude/tracking constraints:** Papers #11, #55, and #57 address attitude convergence or velocity-free attitude control, though they do not directly solve release-to-stop position capture.
